2026

Rainfall Variability and Extreme Event Classification Using Machine Learning in the Narayani Basin, Nepal

First author · Under review, Journal of Water and Climate Change · Code

  • Analyzed 40 years of daily rainfall from 78 gauges; bias-corrected ERA5-Land covariates via empirical quantile mapping, reducing RMSE by up to 61%.
  • Mann-Kendall trend tests and ETCCDI indices: dry spells lengthened at 21 of 77 gauges, shortened at none.
  • Classified extreme deficit vs. excess events with RF, SVM, and XGBoost (RF AUROC 0.803).
